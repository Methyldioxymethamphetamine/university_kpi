President Brief

Standalone screen for the iteration 1 meeting — not a tool view
One cell, Sandip's own 2025 submission, UG placement table
₹37.5L vs ₹3.75L
10× apart — same cell, digits vs. words

A system that reads only the digits reports Sandip's median graduate salary beating IIT Bombay's by nearly two to one. The form requires the amount in words too, and the words agree with every neighbouring year. Nothing in a naive pipeline would ever flag this — the number is perfectly well-formed, just wrong.

Sandip SITRC — NIRF 2025 Overall submission, p.1 ↗ · verified against raw text runs, repeats in 2024 submission
72 vs 56
Distinct metric concepts — Sandip's own published submission carries more than IIT Bombay's public NIRF copy. The institution isn't publishing less; nobody has put the two side by side before.
39 of 72
NIRF concepts with no home in the 229-KPI dictionary — capex breakdowns, consultancy, lateral entry. The dictionary is the thing that's incomplete, not the extraction.
Opposite answers
Campus recycling infrastructure: IIT Bombay reports "comprehensive," Sandip reports "none." Self-declared, directly comparable, and neither institution can game it.
